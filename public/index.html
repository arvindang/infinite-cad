Infinite CAD ∞

CAD your LLM can drive. It writes the part as a tree of named features over MCP, the model renders here live, and you point back at exact faces with links.

  1. Add the MCP server below to Claude (or any MCP client).
  2. Make a model and paste its link into your chat with a task.
  3. Click a face, press ⌘C, paste …#base.top back: “put two M4 holes here.”
